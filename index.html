Android Engineering · Q3 2026Delivery
Challenges

What stretched the timelines

The two biggest delays sat outside development: waiting on product review and waiting on design.

01

Apps stayed in product review, and reviews kept extending

Builds that were ready from engineering waited in product review. Each review round added changes, which pushed the release date further out.

Development Product review Extended Planned release
02

Design clearance and review both waited on designer availability

Design clearance took longer than the development itself. After a short build, design review waited again because the designer was not available for either step.

Design clearance Development Design review Planned release

How AI-assisted design works today

1Developer contextExisting theme, flows, UX research and resources

Main gapAsset discovery. UX can be researched and handed to the AI; finding the right assets is what still needs a person. Output quality depends on the context and resources the developer provides.

Cost optimisation

Lower running costs on Keto and ID Photo

Two optimisations that cut ongoing database and AI costs.

Keto

−70%
Before100%
After~30%

Firestore index size and read/write costs cut by about 70%. A ₹14K credit was secured, with major help from Navfal on the communication. Ongoing cost is estimated at ₹12–13K a month.

ID Photo

−49%
Gemini100%
Grok~51%

AI provider costs compared, then image generation moved from Gemini to Grok for about 49% lower cost.

Idle resources shut downUnused cloud instances on FM Radio and NoteQA were identified and switched off, ending that spend.
InsightSearch grounding queries can cost far more than model tokens. AI choices are now judged on query cost and scalability, not token price alone.
